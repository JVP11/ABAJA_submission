BAJA SAE INDIA 2026 - LKA Performance Metrics

RMSE (Root Mean Square Error)

Formula: RMSE = sqrt((1/N) * sum(tRoad(i)^2))

Where: - tRoad(i) = lateral deviation from lane center (m) - N = total
number of samples

MATLAB: RMSE = sqrt(mean(tRoad.^2));

Computed Result: RMSE = 1.4331 m

ILC (In-Lane Coverage)

Formula: ILC = (Number of samples where |tRoad| <= T) / N * 100

Where: - T = Lane Width / 2 - N = total number of samples

MATLAB: T = 1.75; ILC = sum(abs(tRoad) <= T)/length(tRoad)*100;

Computed Result: ILC = 72.25 %

Methodology

The lateral deviation signal (tRoad) was recorded throughout the
complete LKA run. RMSE was calculated using the root mean square of the
lateral deviation samples. ILC was calculated as the percentage of
samples for which the absolute lateral deviation remained within half of
the lane width (±1.75 m).
